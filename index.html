Who lives up there?

Between 6 to 10 astronauts live and work aboard the ISS continuously, conducting scientific experiments for all humankind.

⚽

How big is the Station?

It's about the size of a full American football field (109 meters end-to-end), weighing over 400,000 kilograms!

👀

Can you see it from your home?

Yes! It looks like a very bright, fast-moving star gliding across the night sky with no blinking lights.
